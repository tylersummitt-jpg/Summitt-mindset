Pat Summitt's Championing Women in Leadership program. Take your leadership skills to the next level with Championing Women in Leadership. Based on the principles of championship-level leader and coach Pat Summitt, this program is online and self-paced, featuring lessons including video, written and other interactive content such as quizzes and a PDF workbook to accompany the course. Designed to support both current and future leaders, the program is engaging and informative for men and women alike.
