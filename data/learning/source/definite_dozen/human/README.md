# Pat Summitt Leadership Group Definite Dozen

Capture status: **in_progress**. JSON is canonical.

Great teams start with a great leader.

Pat Summitt Leadership Group empowers leaders on and off the court to create winning teams using the wisdom and principles of legendary basketball coach, Pat Summitt.

This program harnesses The Definite Dozen principles that were the foundation of Pat Summitt’s winning teams and applies them to leading in the workplace and achieving personal success.

In this series of 14 lessons, you will walk through Pat’s Definite Dozen and gain insights on how you can apply these principles to achieve your goals in life and develop strong relationships along the way.
